# 新增图片的生成提示词

本轮使用内置 imagegen 模式，生成两张独立竖版海报，延续原有纸张拼贴、炭黑文字与朱红批注的风格。

## 04 · 实验与发现

保存位置：`media/images/experiments-to-findings.png`，1086 × 1448。

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: the fourth image in a Chinese academic-writing skill promotional series, standalone portrait poster, 3:4 aspect ratio, high resolution.
Primary request: Make a beautiful editorial poster for AI Paper Review about experiments accumulating without a clear research finding.
Scene/backdrop: warm ivory uncoated paper, subtle grain; restrained charcoal ink and vermilion red. Premium literary-journal / handmade editorial collage aesthetic, matching an existing series of cream paper, black Songti headlines, torn manuscript paper and red proofreader marks.
Composition: generous but purposeful margins, small spaced English masthead at the top; very large beautifully typeset Chinese Songti serif title across the upper half, dramatic asymmetric line lengths. In the lower half, a tactile still-life collage of a small open archival tray overflowing with loose manuscript strips, graphite crosshatching and a dark engraved silhouette of paper. A single vermilion thread is tangled among these strips and leads to a cleaner cream index card marked only with one large hand-drawn red question mark. One restrained red pencil gesture circles the title word 发现. A small footer sits neatly above the bottom edge. Real depth from soft paper shadows. It must feel designed by a human art director with strong typography, not a software infographic.
Text (verbatim, only this readable text):
Top: AI PAPER REVIEW
Headline, line 1: 实验做了很多，
Headline, line 2: 发现
Headline, line 3: 是什么？
Footer: 每个实验，都应回答一个问题。
Constraints: Chinese glyphs must be correct, large, legible and never overlap. Manuscripts contain only abstract faint ink strokes, not real numbers, results or quotations. The paper collage is an artistic metaphor, not a scientific diagram. Keep the cream/charcoal/vermilion palette. No robots, blue gradients, neon, plastic 3D UI, app mockups, badges, extra slogans, decorative borders or watermark.
```
